**mLab AI** is an internship programme run by **mobitouch** from Rzeszów. The 2025 edition was the first in its current form, and members of our club were among the interns.

## What they built

**20 participants** took part and built **6 apps** for non-profits in the Podkarpacie region, including EKOSKOP, the SOS Życie foundation and Parasol. One of them is visible in the stage photo: an app that encourages primary school teachers to run eco-activities with pupils, building habits through challenges and daily research.

## Mentoring from start to finish

The interns had mentors from mobitouch who guided them from requirements to deployment. The office photo shows what that looked like day to day: a conversation, a laptop and quick decisions.
