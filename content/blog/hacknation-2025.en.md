**HackNation 2025** is Poland's first 24-hour implementation hackathon, run by and for public administration. The tasks were prepared by ministries, public institutions and state-owned companies. On 6 and 7 December 2025 around 1,500 people worked in Bydgoszcz, about 50 of them from WSIiZ.

## Two finals

Students and alumni of our university reached the finals in two Ministry of Digital Affairs tasks:

- **"Zgrany budżet"** ("In-sync budget"): a web app that streamlines how the ministry builds its budget. The product scored 4.85/5, and more than 20 projects were submitted in the category.
- **"Odnalezione zguby"** ("Found lost items"): a portal where officials publish information about found items in a uniform, machine-readable format.

## Practice beats slides

Vice-Rector for Education Dr Barbara Przywara put it this way: "There is nothing more practical in education than an event like this." That is how we see hackathons too: a real problem, a team, a deadline and a product at the end.
