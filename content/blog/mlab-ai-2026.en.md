Not everything we do is a hackathon. Several people from our club took part in **mLab AI**, an internship programme that the Rzeszów company **mobitouch** runs for students. The results were shown on 14 October 2026 at the Demo Day in Fabryka Smaku, on the Rzeszów University of Technology campus.

## How mLab works

It is an AI internship programme where interns build real apps for non-profit organisations, and the organisations get them for free. Students work in teams under mentors from mobitouch: from requirements, through code reviews and stand-ups, to deployment. The tracks were AI Developer (.NET, Fullstack, Flutter), AI Product Manager and Marketing & Content.

## The third edition

The 2026 edition is the third round of the programme, the previous one ran in 2025. About 100 people applied and **23 students from five universities** made it in. Over 15 weeks the teams put in more than 6,300 hours, some at the office, some remotely.

## 10 projects for 10 organisations

The teams delivered 10 solutions for non-profits, eight of which work in the Podkarpacie region. Among them:

- **ResQ** for Fundacja Q: a crisis-relief coordination platform,
- **Grantoteka** for Fundacja AS Kier: grant and KSeF invoice accounting,
- a grant competition system for the Opole Centre for Supporting NGO Initiatives,
- **Wirtualne Biuro** (Virtual Office) for the Bieszczady Folk University,
- an app supporting psychotherapy patients for Fundacja Mediculus,
- an AI assistant for parents for Bilingual Future Foundation.

There were also a festival site for Chen Taijiquan with workshop sign-ups and the "Senior w Sieci" app.

## Our people on stage

The certificate photos show programme participants, including people from KNNTP. Internships like this teach what a lecture cannot: a real client, a deadline and responsibility for a product.
