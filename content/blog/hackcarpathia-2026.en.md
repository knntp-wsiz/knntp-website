Not every hackathon needs a trip. **HackCarpathia** takes place in Rzeszów, the city where our club is based. Its third edition was held in April 2026 and is described as the biggest event of its kind in the city.

## Who won

The main prize and the distinction in the Medtech category went to team **CombAIns** from Warsaw University of Technology. They built an app called "Bądź gotowa" ("Be ready") for teenage girls who fear their first visit to a gynaecologist.

The event was covered by local and regional media, including TVP3 Rzeszów, Polskie Radio Rzeszów and nowiny24.pl.

## Why we are writing about it

HackCarpathia is a good chance to try a hackathon without travelling: everything happens right here in Rzeszów.
